  • What these files are

    Section-only explorations. Each sample is a self-contained HTML file with no header, footer or neighbouring sections. Compose them later — do not treat one as a finished page.

  • Compose a full page

    Header × hero × delivery × money × why × work × CTA × footer. The rules for extracting and namespacing the shortlist live in homepage-compositor-prompt.md.

  • Content is locked

    Headline, lead, ₹ figures, checks, captions and disclaimers come from docs/HOMEPAGE-SCREENSHOT-RESEARCH.md. Redesign the layout, never the claims.

  • Preview locally

    python3 -m http.server 8124 from this folder, then open /. Every sample also accepts ?theme=light or ?theme=dark.
