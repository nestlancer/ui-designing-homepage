CRITICAL DESIGNER INSIGHT

Why Professional UI Review Differs from the Automated Compositor Score

The automated generator (tools/review.mjs) scored 48 of the Top 50 pages as an identical 43/45 tie and placed P04 · H08 · D03 · M04 · W05 · S03 · C04 · Ftr-P04 at #1 (44/45). Our 7-tool audit uncovered 6 concrete reasons why real users prefer a different order:

What the Coarse Heuristic (review.mjs) Did

  • Vector Standard Deviation Bias: Because W05 was assigned trait vector [1,1,1], the script rewarded slots with vector [1,0,1] (P04, H08, D03, M04, S03, C04) and penalized clean flat/grid surfaces assigned [0,0,0] (P01, H01, H07, D01, S01, S02, C01) — even though S01 and S02 actually use elevated shadowed cards!
  • Coarse Integer Rounding: Rounding 9 aspect scores to the nearest integer compressed 684 different pages into a single 43/45 tie bucket.
  • No DOM/CSS Inspection of UX Ergonomics: It did not check how many clicks it takes to view portfolio items, whether "Sign in" is visible on desktop, or whether CTA button shapes match.

6 Real UI/UX Defects Our Multi-Tool Suite Caught

  • 1. Missing Desktop "Sign in" in hdr-P02 (12 pages): In P02-glass, <a href="/login">Sign in</a> is only inside the mobile drawer (.nav__panel), which is display:none at >=900px! Desktop visitors have no Sign In button.
  • 2. Portfolio Hiding + Swap Bug + Stale Copy in work-S03 (17 pages): S03 shows only 1 of 8 projects at a time (requiring 7 clicks), boots on item 05/08 instead of 01/08, attaches img.onload before setting img.src (forcing a 320ms timeout delay on every tab click), and leaves the unedited phrase "for a lending platform" in initial #pv-desc HTML.
  • 3. Scale Inversion & Shape Clash in cta-C04 (29 pages): C04 sets its closing H2 to 88px (-0.077em tracking, 0.92 line-height) — 57% larger than the 56px Hero H1 — and uses 3px square stacked buttons while every other section uses 999px pill buttons!
  • 4. Illegible 7.5px Micro-Type & Non-Clickable Rows in work-S10 (4 pages): S10 has 7 CSS rules between 7.5px and 9.5px, requires ~1,400px of scrolling, and has no click handler on .case cards.
  • 5. Non-Semantic Banner & 42px Target in hdr-P04 (11 pages): P04-pill uses <div role="banner"> instead of <header> and sizes its theme button at 42×42px (<44px WCAG target).
  • 6. Light-Mode Dark Slab & Right-Aligned Copy in why-W04 (2 pages): W04 inserts a full-bleed #0b2b36 dark plane into Light Mode (ΔL = 0.89 seam jump) and right-aligns odd proof paragraphs.
